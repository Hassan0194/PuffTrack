# PuffTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

PuffTrack is a personal tracker for nicotine use. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Summary

PuffTrack keeps your data on your device. It does not have accounts, does not use analytics or advertising, and does not send your data to any server.

## What the app stores

The following is stored locally on your device only:

- Your answers from setup (product type, usual daily amount, nicotine strength, how long you have used it, your goal, and when you last used it).
- Each entry you log, with its time and optional context (for example "Stress" or "Coffee").
- Your daily limit, personal target, and reduction goal.
- Your optional daily reminder time.

## What leaves your device

Nothing is sent automatically. Data leaves your device only when you choose to:

- **Export a backup or PDF report.** The file is handed to the Android share sheet. You decide where it goes (for example Files, Drive, or a message). PuffTrack does not see or control where you send it.
- **Import a backup.** The file you pick is read on your device and is not uploaded anywhere.

## Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, PuffTrack asks for notification permission and schedules a local notification on your device. No server is involved.

## Permissions

- **Notifications:** only used for the daily reminder you turn on.
- **Files:** PuffTrack reads a file only when you pick one to import.

## Your control

- You can export your data at any time from Settings.
- You can delete all PuffTrack data by clearing the app's storage in your phone settings or uninstalling the app. Because data is stored only on your device, it cannot be recovered after that unless you saved a backup.

## Health information

PuffTrack is a tracking tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. The daily limit is a tracking boundary you choose, not a medical recommendation. Talk to a doctor or a local health service for advice about nicotine use.

## Children

PuffTrack is intended for adults. It is not directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with the app and the date above will change.
