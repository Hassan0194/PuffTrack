## Full description
PuffTrack is a simple, private tracker for people who want a clear picture of their nicotine use.

Pick what you use: vape, cigarettes, or nicotine pouches. Log an entry with one tap, from the app or from the home-screen widget.

**Track**
- One-tap logging, with undo for mistakes
- A live gauge of today against your daily limit
- Tag entries with a context like Stress, Coffee or Work

**Understand**
- Daily history and a 7-day view
- Insights: your busiest time of day, most common trigger, longest gap between entries, and this week against last week
- Streaks and milestones for days on track

**Set your own limits**
- A daily limit you control, with sensible defaults
- An optional personal target and reduction goal, kept separate from the limit

**Private by design**
- All data stays on your device
- No account, no ads, no analytics
- Export a backup or a PDF report any time, and restore from a backup

**Home-screen widget**
Log an entry without opening the app.

PuffTrack is a tracking tool. It does not give medical advice, and the daily limit is a tracking boundary you choose, not a medical recommendation.
